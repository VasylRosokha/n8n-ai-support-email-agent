# Order tracking and order changes

## Order statuses

Every order goes through these statuses:

1. **Received** – payment is complete, the order is waiting for the next roast day
2. **Roasting** – your coffee is being roasted and packed
3. **Shipped** – the parcel is with the carrier
4. **Delivered** – the carrier has delivered the parcel

You can see the status in your account under **Orders**. If you ordered as a guest, use the link in your order confirmation email.

## Tracking your parcel

When your order ships, we email you the tracking number and a link to the carrier: PostNL in the Netherlands, DHL everywhere else. The first tracking scan can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Tracking hasn't updated

If the tracking hasn't changed for 3 business days (EU) or 5 business days (outside the EU), email us your order number. We contact the carrier and get back to you.

## Lost parcels

A parcel counts as lost when it hasn't been delivered 10 business days after dispatch within the EU, or 20 business days after dispatch outside the EU. In that case we open a claim with the carrier and send you a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Parcel marked as delivered but not received

Wait until the end of the next business day, and check with neighbours and any safe place the carrier may have used. If the parcel still hasn't turned up, email us your order number and we start an investigation with the carrier.

## Changing or cancelling an order

While an order has the status **Received**, we can change the delivery address or the items, or cancel it for free. Email us your order number as soon as possible. Once the status is **Roasting** or **Shipped**, the order can't be changed or cancelled; you can return it under our returns policy instead.

## Order confirmation not received

The confirmation email is sent within a few minutes of ordering. Check your spam folder first. If it hasn't arrived after an hour, email us and we'll send it again.
