# Wholesale and business orders

## Who wholesale is for

We supply cafés, restaurants, hotels, offices and shops in the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany and Luxembourg. We don't offer wholesale to businesses in other countries at the moment. We don't offer private-label coffee.

## Minimum order and prices

The minimum wholesale order is 5 kg of coffee per delivery. Wholesale prices depend on your monthly volume. We don't publish them; you receive a price list once your application is approved.

## How to apply for a wholesale account

Email wholesale@northwindcoffee.example with:

- business name and address
- VAT number
- type of business (café, office, shop, …)
- estimated monthly volume in kg

We reply within 2 business days. Approved cafés and restaurants get a free sample pack of 3 × 250 g.

## Wholesale delivery and payment terms

Wholesale orders are delivered free from 10 kg; smaller orders pay the standard shipping price. The first 3 orders are paid in advance by bank transfer or card. After that, you can pay by invoice within 14 days.

## Coffee for offices

Offices can order from 5 kg per month on a fixed monthly delivery. We can also supply grinders and brewers for the office at wholesale prices.

## Barista training

Wholesale cafés and restaurants get a free introductory barista training session at our roastery in Rotterdam. We arrange the date after your first order.
