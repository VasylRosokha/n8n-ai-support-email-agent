# Contact and opening hours

## Customer support by email

Email support@northwindcoffee.example. We answer within 1 business day. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00 Central European Time. Emails sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day. Please include your order number if your question is about an order.

## Phone and chat

We don't offer phone or live chat support. Email is the fastest way to reach us.

## Wholesale contact

Questions about wholesale and business orders go to wholesale@northwindcoffee.example.

## Roastery pickup hours

Our roastery in Rotterdam is open on Saturdays from 10:00 to 15:00 for picking up online orders. There is no walk-in shop or café, so products can't be bought on the spot.

## Public holidays

We're closed on Dutch public holidays. We don't roast or ship on those days, and support replies on the next business day. Before Christmas we announce the last roast day on our website.

## Newsletter

Sign up for the newsletter at the bottom of our website. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link at the bottom.
