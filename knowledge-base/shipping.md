# Shipping

## Countries we ship to

We ship from our roastery in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, to:

- the Netherlands
- all European Union countries
- the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway
- the United States and Canada

We don't ship to any other countries at the moment, for example Australia, Japan or countries in South America. We can't deliver to P.O. boxes.

## Shipping prices and delivery times

| Destination | Shipping price | Free shipping from | Delivery time after dispatch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Netherlands | €4.95 | €40 | 1–2 business days |
| Belgium, Germany, Luxembourg | €6.95 | €60 | 2–3 business days |
| Other EU countries | €8.95 | €75 | 3–6 business days |
| United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway | €14.95 | no free shipping | 4–8 business days |
| United States, Canada | €19.95 | no free shipping | 7–12 business days |

The free shipping threshold applies to the order total, including VAT. Subscription deliveries have their own shipping rules, described in the subscription documentation.

## When orders are dispatched

We roast coffee on Mondays and Thursdays so every bag ships fresh.

- Coffee ordered by Sunday 23:59 is roasted on Monday and dispatched on Monday or Tuesday.
- Coffee ordered by Wednesday 23:59 is roasted on Thursday and dispatched on Thursday or Friday.
- Orders with only equipment (no coffee) are dispatched within 1 business day.

Delivery times in the shipping table count from the day of dispatch, not the day of the order.

## Express delivery

Express delivery is available to the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany for €12.95. It delivers the parcel 1 business day after dispatch. Express doesn't change roast days: coffee still ships after the next roast day.

## Customs duties and taxes outside the EU

Orders shipped to the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, the United States or Canada can be charged import duties, taxes or customs fees by the destination country. The recipient pays these charges; they are not included in our prices and we can't pay them on your behalf. Orders shipped outside the EU are charged without Dutch VAT.

## Carriers

Parcels within the Netherlands are delivered by PostNL. Parcels to all other countries are delivered by DHL. You receive a tracking link by email when your order is dispatched.

## Pickup at the roastery in Rotterdam

You can pick up online orders for free at our roastery in Rotterdam on Saturdays from 10:00 to 15:00. Choose **Pickup** at checkout. We email you when the order is ready; bring your order number. The pickup address is in your order confirmation email.
