# Coffee subscription

## How the subscription works

Choose a coffee (or **Roaster's choice**, which changes every delivery), a bag size, a grind and a delivery frequency. Each delivery is roasted on the next roast day after its order date and shipped right after. Your account shows the date of the next order under **Subscriptions → Next order**. Each delivery is charged on its order date.

## Subscription plans and prices

| Plan | Price per delivery |
|---|---|
| 1 × 250 g | €12.00 |
| 2 × 250 g | €22.00 |
| 1 × 1 kg | €38.00 |

Delivery frequency: every 2, 4 or 6 weeks. There is no minimum term and no sign-up fee.

## Subscription shipping

Subscription deliveries ship free to the Netherlands and all EU countries. Deliveries to the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, the United States and Canada are charged the standard shipping price for that country on every delivery.

## Changing your subscription

Log in and open **Subscriptions** to change the coffee, bag size, grind, frequency, delivery address or next order date. Changes made at least 2 days before the next order date apply to that order. Later changes apply from the order after it.

## Pausing or skipping a delivery

In **Subscriptions** you can skip the next delivery or pause the subscription for up to 3 months. A paused subscription restarts automatically on the date you chose, and you get a reminder email 3 days before.

## Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel at any time in **Subscriptions → Cancel subscription**, with no fee. To stop the next delivery, cancel at least 2 days before its order date. If that order has already been charged, it will still be roasted and shipped.

## Paying for a subscription

Subscriptions can be paid by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) or PayPal. Update your payment method in **Subscriptions → Payment**. If a payment fails, we try again after 2 days. After 3 failed attempts the subscription is paused and we email you.
