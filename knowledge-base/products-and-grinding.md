## Grind options

Every coffee is available as whole beans or ground for:

- Espresso
- Moka pot
- AeroPress
- Filter / pour-over
- French press
- Cold brew

Choose the grind on the product page or in your subscription. Every grind costs the same. We recommend whole beans if you have a grinder, because they stay fresh longer. The grind can't be changed once an order has the status **Roasting**.

## Freshness and storage

The roast date is printed on every bag. Filter coffee tastes best 4 days to 6 weeks after roasting; espresso is best from 7 days after roasting. Unopened bags keep well for 3 months thanks to the one-way valve. After opening, keep the coffee in its bag or an airtight container, somewhere cool and dark. Don't store coffee in the fridge.
