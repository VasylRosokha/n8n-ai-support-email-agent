# Payment

## Accepted payment methods

We accept:

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- iDEAL (Netherlands)
- Bancontact (Belgium)

Subscriptions can only be paid by card or PayPal.

## Currency

All prices are in euros (EUR). If your card is in another currency, your card provider converts the amount and may charge a fee.

## Pay later, instalments and cash on delivery

We don't offer pay-later, instalment plans or cash on delivery. Payment by bank transfer is only available for wholesale accounts.

## VAT and invoices

Prices for EU customers include Dutch VAT. Every order confirmation email includes a PDF invoice. EU businesses can enter their VAT number at checkout: with a valid VAT number from outside the Netherlands, the order is charged without VAT (reverse charge). Orders shipped outside the EU are charged without Dutch VAT.

## Failed payments

An order is placed only once the payment succeeds. Common reasons for a failed payment are a card declined by the bank, an incomplete 3-D Secure check, or an expired card. Try again, or use another payment method. We never see or store your full card details; payments are handled by our payment provider.

## Charged twice

A failed payment attempt can show up as a pending amount on your account. Your bank releases it automatically, usually within 5 business days. If you see two completed charges for the same order, email us your order number and we refund the duplicate.

## Discount codes

Discount codes are only sent through our newsletter. Customer support can't create discount codes, and a code can't be added to an order after it has been placed. We don't price-match other shops.
