# About Northwind Coffee

## Who we are

Northwind Coffee is a small specialty coffee roastery in Rotterdam, the Netherlands, founded in 2019. A team of six roasts every coffee in small batches and ships it straight from the roastery.

## Where our coffee comes from

We buy green coffee through importers who work directly with farms and cooperatives in Brazil, Colombia and Ethiopia. We pay above the Fairtrade minimum price for every coffee we buy.

## Organic and Fairtrade certification

Harbour Espresso and Colombia Huila are certified organic (EU organic). Our other coffees aren't certified organic. We aren't Fairtrade certified, even though we pay above the Fairtrade minimum price.

## Packaging

Our coffee bags are made of paper with a plant-based lining and a one-way valve. They can go in industrial compost where your local waste service accepts compostable packaging; otherwise put them in general waste. Shipping boxes are recycled cardboard and can be recycled with paper.
