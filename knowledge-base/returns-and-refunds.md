# Returns and refunds

## Returning equipment

You can return unused equipment in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery. You pay the return shipping. We refund the item price once the return has arrived and been checked.

## Returning coffee

Unopened coffee bags can be returned within 14 days of delivery. You pay the return shipping. For food safety reasons we can't accept opened coffee, unless it was damaged or not what you ordered.

## Damaged, faulty or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. You can choose a free replacement or a full refund, including the original shipping cost. You don't need to send damaged coffee back.

## Equipment warranty

All equipment we sell has a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. The warranty doesn't cover normal wear, damage from misuse, or damage from not descaling. For a warranty claim, email us your order number, a description of the problem and a photo or short video. We repair, replace or refund the item.

## How to start a return

Email support@northwindcoffee.example with your order number and the items you want to return. We reply within 1 business day with the return address and instructions. Please don't send a parcel back before contacting us. We provide a prepaid return label only for faulty, damaged or wrong items.

## When you get your refund

We issue refunds within 5 business days after the return arrives and has been checked. The money goes back to the original payment method; your bank or card provider may need another 3–5 business days to show it. Original shipping costs are refunded only when the item was faulty, damaged or wrong.

## Returning subscription deliveries

Coffee from a subscription follows the same rules as any other coffee: unopened bags can be returned within 14 days of delivery, and damaged or wrong bags are replaced or refunded. To stop future deliveries, cancel or pause the subscription in your account.
